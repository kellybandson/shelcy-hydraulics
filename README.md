# SHELCY HYDRAULICS

**Hydraulic Sales, Repairs & Engineering Services**

## About Us

Shelcy Hydraulics is a Harare-based hydraulic sales, repair and engineering services company dedicated to providing reliable, efficient and quality solutions to the mining, construction, agricultural and industrial sectors.

We specialise in hydraulic hose supply and assembly, hydraulic pump, motor, valve and cylinder repairs, as well as general engineering services. Our goal is to keep our customers' machinery and equipment operating efficiently through quality workmanship and dependable service.

## Our Services

- Hydraulic hose supply, manufacturing and repairs.
- Hydraulic pump and motor repairs.
- Hydraulic cylinder repairs and servicing.
- Hydraulic valve repairs.
- Excavator and grader hydraulic services.
- Lathe machining and line boring.
- General engineering and mechanical repairs.
- Hydraulic components and spare parts supply.
